Hollis Wedding Bells

Pete Hollis and Angelina Hutto are joining in the holiest of matrimony this October. From neighbors, to friends, Pete and Angelina found comfort and peace with each other. They soon began dating and in October of 2024 Peter proposed to Angelina on stage at the Bourbon Bandstand in the French Quarter in New Orleans. As the live band played "Caught up in the Rapture" by Anita Baker, Peter proposed.
